# VerityHire

**Hire with confidence. Trust the proof.**

VerityHire is a B2B talent-assessment platform concept designed to help companies identify candidates whose demonstrated skills match the requirements of a specific job role. It combines role-specific assessments, proctoring and evidence-based results to support more consistent hiring decisions while treating candidate privacy as a core product principle.

> **Product principle:** Assess skills fairly, protect candidate data, and help employers make better-informed decisions.

## The problem

Hiring teams often have to make decisions using resumes, self-reported skills, inconsistent interviews, and assessments that may not reflect the work a candidate will actually do. Remote assessments can also create concerns about unauthorized assistance, while poorly designed data practices can expose sensitive candidate information.

VerityHire aims to help employers address these challenges by making assessment results more relevant, consistent, integrity-aware, and easier to interpret.

## Our approach

VerityHire is intended to help employers:

- **Assess for the role:** Define evaluations around the skills and competencies required for a particular job profile.
- **Protect assessment integrity:** Use proportionate proctoring and integrity signals to deter and flag potential misconduct.
- **Make evidence-based decisions:** Present structured results that help hiring teams compare candidates against relevant criteria.
- **Respect candidate privacy:** Collect only necessary information, restrict access, and handle assessment data responsibly.

VerityHire supports hiring decisions; it should not be treated as a guarantee of job performance or as a substitute for human judgment.

## Core capabilities

The capabilities below describe the intended product direction. Actual availability depends on implementation.

### 1. Role-specific assessments

- Create or configure assessments for a specific job profile.
- Define skills, competencies, evaluation criteria, and scoring rules.
- Support different assessment formats as appropriate to the role, such as multiple-choice questions, coding tasks, written responses, or practical exercises.
- Keep assessments relevant to the skills the role actually requires.

### 2. Proctoring and assessment integrity

- Apply assessment rules and communicate them clearly before a candidate begins.
- Use configurable proctoring controls and integrity signals appropriate to the assessment.
- Flag suspicious events for review instead of assuming that every signal proves misconduct.
- Provide a review process for disputed or ambiguous integrity flags.

**Important:** No remote proctoring method can guarantee that cheating is impossible. VerityHire's aim is to deter misconduct, identify potential integrity risks, and support fair review.

### 3. Candidate evaluation and shortlisting

- Present assessment outcomes in a structured format.
- Show performance against job-relevant criteria, not just a single unexplained score.
- Help recruiters build shortlists using transparent, configurable requirements.
- Keep a human reviewer involved in consequential hiring decisions.

### 4. Employer workspace

Potential employer features include:

- Company and team workspaces.
- Job-profile and assessment management.
- Candidate invitations and assessment status tracking.
- Results review and shortlist management.
- Role-based access for recruiters, hiring managers, and administrators.

### 5. Candidate experience

- Clear instructions, assessment requirements, and privacy information.
- Accessible and mobile-conscious user experience where supported.
- Clear communication about the use of proctoring and integrity checks.
- A contact or appeal path for technical issues or disputed results.

## How it works

1. **Define the role** — The employer specifies the job profile and the skills that matter.
2. **Configure the assessment** — The employer selects relevant tasks, scoring criteria, time limits, and integrity settings.
3. **Invite candidates** — Candidates receive the assessment details and applicable privacy and proctoring notices.
4. **Complete the assessment** — Candidates undertake the evaluation under the stated rules.
5. **Review results** — The platform presents skills-based outcomes and any integrity signals that require human review.
6. **Build a shortlist** — The employer evaluates results alongside other relevant hiring evidence and makes the final decision.

## Who it serves

### Employers and hiring teams

Companies that want structured, role-relevant assessments to help screen applicants and prioritize candidates for further evaluation.

### Recruiters and talent-acquisition teams

Teams seeking a consistent way to administer assessments, review outcomes, and coordinate shortlisting.

### Candidates

Applicants who want a clear opportunity to demonstrate their abilities against stated job requirements, with responsible handling of their data.

## Trust, integrity, and privacy

Trust is a product requirement, not just a marketing message. The intended design should include the following safeguards, subject to implementation and applicable law.

- **Data minimization:** Collect only the personal data needed for a defined purpose.
- **Purpose limitation:** Explain why information is collected and how it will be used.
- **Access control:** Restrict candidate records and assessment evidence to authorized users.
- **Secure handling:** Use appropriate security controls for data in transit, at rest, and during processing.
- **Retention controls:** Define retention periods and deletion procedures for candidate records and proctoring evidence.
- **Transparency:** Tell candidates when proctoring is used, what it may observe, and how results are reviewed.
- **Human review:** Treat automated flags as signals for investigation, not automatic proof of cheating.
- **Fairness and accessibility:** Test assessments and integrity controls for avoidable bias, accessibility barriers, and false positives.
- **Incident response:** Establish processes for investigating and communicating suspected data or security incidents.

VerityHire should not claim that candidate data is completely risk-free or that cheating is impossible. Security and integrity controls reduce risk but cannot eliminate it entirely.

## Product principles

1. **Skills over assumptions** — Evaluate demonstrated capability against clearly defined role requirements.
2. **Integrity without overreach** — Use proportionate controls and review evidence fairly.
3. **Privacy by design** — Minimize data collection and limit access from the start.
4. **Explainable outcomes** — Make assessment criteria and results understandable to employers and candidates.
5. **Human accountability** — Keep final hiring decisions accountable to people.
6. **Enterprise readiness** — Build toward reliability, access control, auditability, and responsible administration.

## Roadmap

This is a proposed roadmap, not a statement that these capabilities are already live.

### Phase 1 — Assessment foundation

- Employer workspace and job-profile management.
- Assessment creation and candidate invitations.
- Scoring rules and structured results.
- Basic access control and candidate-data lifecycle policies.

### Phase 2 — Integrity and review

- Configurable proctoring options.
- Integrity-event review workflow.
- Candidate support and dispute-handling process.
- Monitoring for false positives and usability issues.

### Phase 3 — Enterprise workflows

- Team-level permissions and administrative controls.
- Shortlisting and collaboration tools.
- Reporting on assessment performance and hiring-pipeline outcomes.
- Integration planning for employer applicant-tracking systems, subject to customer needs.

### Phase 4 — Quality and scale

- Assessment quality validation and role-specific benchmarks.
- Accessibility and fairness evaluations.
- Operational monitoring, incident response, and reliability improvements.
- Additional integrations driven by validated customer demand.

## Success metrics

Potential product metrics include:

- Employer activation and repeat usage.
- Time from job-profile setup to completed assessments.
- Assessment completion and abandonment rates.
- Hiring-team satisfaction with the relevance of assessment results.
- Relationship between assessment performance and later job-relevant outcomes, where measurable and appropriate.
- Integrity-flag review outcomes, including confirmed issues and false positives.
- Candidate satisfaction, accessibility issues, and privacy-related requests.
- Security incidents and compliance with data-retention policies.

Metrics should be interpreted carefully. For example, more proctoring flags do not automatically mean better integrity, and assessment scores should be validated before being used as hiring predictors.
